# F027: Anonymous chat with PIN protection

- **Status:** Done
- **Branch:** Not created
- **Pull request:** Not created

## Goal

Let two signed-in users chat under chosen aliases after one shares a private invitation URL manually. Each participant uses a six-digit PIN for that conversation. Account identities remain known to the server for access control but are hidden in the chat UI.

## User flow

1. A signed-in user creates a short-lived invitation URL and shares it outside the app. The app sends no invitation email.
2. The recipient signs in, opens the URL, chooses an alias, and accepts or declines. The creator chooses an alias too. Neither can message before acceptance.
3. Each participant sets their own six-digit PIN for this conversation and enters it to open the thread. They can refresh to pull the latest messages or lock the chat immediately. Four wrong attempts cause a 25-minute lockout; each subsequent lockout doubles the duration, capped at six hours.
4. A participant can delete this conversation or **Delete all my anonymous chats**. Either action deletes the affected conversations and messages for both participants after an explicit confirmation.
5. A forgotten PIN cannot be reset. The participant abandons or deletes the conversation and creates a new invitation to chat again.

## In scope

- Single-use, unguessable invitation URLs with expiry, server-side rate limits, and explicit acceptance. A link may be claimed by one signed-in account other than its creator. Its possession grants only the ability to accept or decline, not access to messages.
- Alias-only participant labels in the chat UI. Do not expose account names or email addresses through chat responses.
- A separate hashed PIN verifier, failed-attempt count, and lockout state for each participant in each conversation. Unlock state is short-lived and scoped to that participant and conversation.
- A **Refresh messages** button in each open chat and a **Lock** button that revokes only the caller's unlock for that chat. Refresh never unlocks a locked chat.
- Participant-only message reads and sends after PIN unlock. Revoked or deleted conversations remain inaccessible.
- Delete-one and delete-all controls with clear warnings that deletion affects both participants. Keep a minimal deletion record containing the two email IDs and volume of data deleted for 30 days, then expire it.
- Clear states for pending, accepted, declined, expired, invalid link, incorrect PIN, lockout, and empty chat.

## Out of scope

- Sending chat invitations by email, account search, contact lists, blocking or invitation opt-out, Family chat (F024), attachments, end-to-end encryption, and recovering a forgotten PIN.
- Anonymity from the service operator, infrastructure logs, or someone who learns an identity outside the app.

## UI changes

Add **Anonymous Chat** with **Create invite link**, pending invitations, active chats, alias settings, a conversation PIN gate, message thread, **Refresh messages** and **Lock** controls, and deletion controls. Show invite expiry and a warning that anyone with the link can claim it while signed in. Never promise deletion leaves “no traces”; explain the 30-day minimal deletion record.

## API changes (proposal)

- `POST /api/chat/invitations`: create an expiring, single-use link for the signed-in creator. Store only a hash of its token.
- `POST /api/chat/invitations/{token}/accept` and `/decline`: require a signed-in non-creator; atomically consume the token. Expired, consumed, or invalid links return the same neutral error.
- `POST /api/chat/conversations/{id}/pin` and `/unlock`: set and verify the caller's PIN for that conversation, with server-side attempt limits and lockout.
- `POST /api/chat/conversations/{id}/lock`: immediately revoke the caller's unlock for that conversation without affecting the other participant.
- `GET /api/chat/conversations` and `GET` / `POST /api/chat/conversations/{id}/messages`: require participation; message access also requires a valid conversation unlock.
- `DELETE /api/chat/conversations/{id}` and `DELETE /api/chat/conversations`: delete one or all of the caller's conversations for both participants, with the 30-day deletion record.
- Do not expose account identity, PIN hashes, invitation tokens, or another participant's conversations in responses or logs.

## MongoDB changes (proposal)

Store invitation creator ID, token hash, expiry and claim state; conversation participant IDs and aliases; per-participant conversation PIN hashes, attempt counts and lockout state; and messages. Use atomic claim and deletion operations so a link cannot create duplicate conversations and deleted messages cannot reappear. Store only both email IDs and counts/bytes deleted in the deletion record with a 30-day TTL; no message content. Never store a raw PIN or invitation token.

## Architecture decisions

- “Anonymous” describes identities shown to participants, not access to the service. Normal authentication is always required.
- A PIN belongs to one participant in one conversation. Participants do not share PINs, and there is no PIN reset.
- Lockout duration after the fourth wrong attempt is 25 minutes, then 50, 100, 200, and at most 360 minutes for subsequent lockouts. A successful unlock clears the failed-attempt count but does not weaken normal authentication.
- Deletion removes conversation data for both participants; the minimal deletion record expires after 30 days.
- An authenticated participant may delete a conversation without its PIN, so a forgotten PIN does not trap it. Message access and alias changes still require unlocking.
- Keep F027 separate from F024. Family membership grants no chat access.

## Acceptance criteria

- A manually shared link can be accepted only once by a signed-in non-creator before expiry; no invitation email is sent.
- Neither participant can exchange messages before acceptance, and the UI shows only aliases.
- A third account cannot enumerate or read invitations, conversations, or messages. A leaked link does not expose existing messages.
- Each conversation requires its own PIN for each participant. Four incorrect entries lock it for 25 minutes; repeated lockouts increase to a six-hour cap and survive reloads.
- Refresh loads messages sent by the other participant without another unlock while the caller's unlock is active. Lock immediately hides messages, invalidates the prior unlock token on the server, and requires the PIN again; other conversations and the other participant remain unaffected.
- A forgotten PIN has no recovery path. Deleting one or all conversations removes them for both participants while retaining only the specified 30-day deletion record.

## Local verification

With two browser sessions and an unrelated account, check link claim and expiry, acceptance, alias display, participant isolation, independent per-conversation PINs, escalating lockout, and deletion for both participants. Inspect the deletion record and its TTL.

## Owner decisions

1. Invitation URLs are shared manually; the app sends no chat invitation email.
2. PINs are per conversation. A forgotten PIN means abandoning or deleting that conversation and starting again.
3. Repeated lockouts increase up to six hours.
4. Delete-all affects both participants; keep only email IDs and deletion volume for 30 days.
5. Invitation blocking and opt-out are unnecessary for this URL-based flow.
